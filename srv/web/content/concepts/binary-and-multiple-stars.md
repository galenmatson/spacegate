# Binary and Multiple Stars

Many stars belong to gravitationally bound systems. A binary contains two accepted stellar members; a multiple contains three or more. Complex systems are commonly hierarchical: close pairs orbit within wider pairs or groups.

Hierarchy matters because there may be no useful single orbit that places every member around one central star. Astronomers publish orbital solutions for particular pairs or subsystems, often using different observing methods and epochs.

## What the tags mean

Spacegate's **Binary** and **Multiple** tags count accepted stellar members. **Hierarchical** requires nested structure in the accepted public hierarchy. These tags do not claim that every orbital element is known or that a proposed configuration has been proven stable over its entire lifetime.
