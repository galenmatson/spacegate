# Spectral Class

A stellar spectrum spreads light into wavelength bands. Dark absorption lines and bright emission lines show how atoms and ions interact with radiation in a star's atmosphere. The broad sequence **O, B, A, F, G, K, M** is primarily a temperature sequence; the later **L, T, and Y** classes extend into ultracool stars and brown dwarfs.

Class is not destiny. An M-class spectrum can belong to a small main-sequence red dwarf or an evolved red giant. Luminosity class, surface gravity, composition, variability, and other evidence are needed to distinguish those objects.
