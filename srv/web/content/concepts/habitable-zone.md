# Habitable Zones

A habitable zone is a modeled range of stellar energy where a suitable planet could maintain liquid water at its surface. Its location depends strongly on stellar luminosity and on the atmospheric model used to define the inner and outer limits.

Being in this range is not proof of habitability. Planet mass, atmosphere, pressure, clouds, rotation, magnetic environment, geological history, and the behavior of the host star all matter. A gas giant in the zone is not an Earth analog, though its moons may be interesting targets.

Spacegate therefore labels this result **HZ Screen**. It is an invitation to inspect a system, not a verdict about life.
