# Orbital Period

Orbital period is the time required for one complete orbit. It is often one of the best-measured parameters for transiting planets, eclipsing binaries, and repeating radial-velocity signals.

Period alone does not describe an orbit. Eccentricity describes its shape, inclination describes orientation relative to a reference plane, and a semimajor axis describes scale. In multiple systems, each published solution also applies to a particular pair or subsystem.

Spacegate tags a confirmed planet **USP** when its selected period is shorter than one day. That is a deterministic screening threshold. The selected period still points back to its evidence record and uncertainty.
